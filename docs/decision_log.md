## Future enhancements

- **A. Keyword-index whole tables.** Add the table's rows (`context`) to `fts` for table chunks. Free; a migration. Only keyword search improves, and long numeric tables may crowd keyword results.
- **B. Summaries that name the row items** (e.g. "Adverse reactions in ≥5% of patients: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea…", still no numbers). ~$0.30 to re-summarize 1,455 tables and re-embed. Improves keyword and dense search and keeps chunks short. Preferred.
